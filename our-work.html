Our work

Change that stays
after we leave

We partner with governments to strengthen education leadership, improve teaching and learning, and build public education systems that create lasting impact for every child.

Our approach

Strengthening public education systems across India

Mantra4Change's journey across states is rooted in one belief: lasting change in education happens when systems are strengthened from within. Working alongside state governments, academic institutions and education leaders, we build long-term partnerships that improve leadership, teaching practices and learning experiences at scale.

Our work focuses on

Education leadership

Strengthening school leaders and education officials through leadership development programmes, peer learning spaces and continuous capacity building.

Teaching and learning

Supporting teachers with innovative pedagogies such as Project Based Learning and other evidence-based approaches that improve classroom experiences and student outcomes.

System strengthening

Working with governments, SCERTs, DIETs and education institutions to strengthen policies, implementation frameworks and institutional capacity.

Community engagement

Building stronger connections between schools and communities through Parent Teacher Meetings, Shiksha Chaupals and other participatory initiatives.

Our footprint

Six states, one method

Our footprint reflects more than the programmes we have run. It reflects stronger institutions, empowered educators, engaged communities, and systems increasingly equipped to lead their own transformation.

Select a state to see what has changed there.

Leadership and institutions

We have strengthened school leadership through capacity-building programmes and peer learning spaces, enabling education leaders to drive meaningful school improvement. Partnering with SCERTs, DIETs and Teacher Education Institutes, we have supported institutional reforms that build the capacity of teachers and school leaders — creating systems that keep growing independently.

Classrooms and outcomes

We have introduced and scaled Project Based Learning across government schools, helping teachers create engaging learning experiences while improving outcomes in mathematics, science and English. Through PACE in Punjab, we have also supported students preparing for competitive examinations like JEE and NEET, widening access to higher education.

Families and communities

From enabling Bihar's first statewide Parent-Teacher Meeting to integrating PTM data into Telangana's state systems and nurturing women-led community platforms through Shiksha Chaupals, our work has created real avenues for families and communities to take part in children's education.
