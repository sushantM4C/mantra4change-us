Improving public education in India is only possible when we all come together

Shikshagraha is that coming together — a nationwide movement of teachers, parents, youth and citizens acting on a shared belief. Mantra4Change anchors it.

Since it began, the movement has grown into a coalition working across 16 states and 47 districts, logging more than 1.37 million small improvements inside schools. Every contribution adds up once enough people decide a school is their responsibility too.

Contributions fund the programmes Mantra4Change runs with state governments in India — school leadership development, teacher capacity building, Project Based Learning and community engagement.
