The challenge

Why India's education system needs strengthening

India is home to one of the world's largest public education systems, with over 121.6 million children enrolled in government schools and more than 10 million teachers shaping the future of the next generation. Yet ensuring that every child receives a quality education remains a complex challenge across the country's diverse social, cultural and geographic contexts.

While access to education has improved significantly, learning outcomes continue to demand attention. According to ASER 2024, many children are still unable to achieve age-appropriate foundational reading and arithmetic skills — highlighting the need to not only bring children to school, but to make sure they keep learning and thrive once they are there.

About Mantra4Change India

Strengthening the system, not just schools

Lasting change happens when the education system itself becomes stronger. Rather than working around the system, we work with it — partnering with governments to strengthen teacher capacity, school leadership, teacher education institutions and community participation.

InspireStep one

Nurture the wider ecosystem

We weave a network of civil society organisations through Shikshagraha, amplifying the focus on school improvement through shared resources, innovative programmes and collective action.

EnableStep two

Build capacity inside government

We partner with education departments at district, state and national levels — including SCERTs and DIETs — to co-create solutions that improve systemic structures, processes and school-level practice.

TransformStep three

Equip leaders to improve from within

Education leaders at every level learn to identify challenges, make data-informed decisions and drive contextually relevant solutions — so improvement continues long after our direct involvement ends.

Initiatives that make this real

School Leadership Development

Head teachers and education officials build the skills to lead school improvement.

Teacher Capacity Building

Continuous professional development that reaches teachers where they already are.

Project Based Learning

Classroom practice that improves outcomes in mathematics, science and English.

System Strengthening

Policy, planning and institutional capacity built with SCERTs, DIETs and departments.

About Mantra4Change US

Connecting global support to local change

Mantra4Change US extends this mission by mobilising a global community of supporters who believe every child deserves access to quality education. By connecting individuals, philanthropists, foundations and organisations with high-impact education initiatives in India, we help raise resources, foster strategic partnerships and amplify collective action to strengthen public education at scale.

Every contribution supports long-term, sustainable improvements that empower educators, strengthen institutions and create better learning opportunities for millions of children.
