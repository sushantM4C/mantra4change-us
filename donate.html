Isolated interventions don't hold

Every child deserves access to quality education, but lasting change cannot happen through isolated interventions alone. At Mantra4Change, we work alongside governments, educators and communities to strengthen the public education system from within — so improvements continue long after our direct involvement ends.

Your support helps create systems that reach millions of children, empower educators and build stronger schools across India.

Choose the scale of your impact

Because we work through the public system, a gift doesn't buy one classroom's supplies — it strengthens the people responsible for many.

$1,000 Empower a classroom

Puts Project Based Learning into the hands of a teacher and their students for a full year.

 $10,000 Empower a school

Supports a head teacher through leadership development and school-wide improvement planning.

 $50,000 Empower a district

Backs district officials and block education officers driving improvement across hundreds of schools.

 $150,000 Empower a state

Anchors a state-level partnership with SCERTs, DIETs and the education department.

Six reasons this approach works

Systemic change that lasts

We partner with governments to strengthen leadership, teaching practices and institutions, enabling education systems to sustain and scale change independently.

Impact at scale

Our work reaches thousands of schools, educators and education leaders across multiple states, creating better learning opportunities for millions of children.

Evidence-driven solutions

From Project Based Learning to school leadership development, every programme is designed, tested and refined using research, data and continuous learning.

Strong government partnerships

Working with state governments, SCERTs, DIETs and education departments lets successful models become part of the system rather than standalone projects.

Communities at the centre

Through Parent Teacher Meetings, Shiksha Chaupals and community engagement programmes, we bring families and local communities into children's learning journeys.

Responsible stewardship

We are committed to transparency, accountability and the responsible use of every contribution, ensuring your support creates measurable impact.

What your support funds

Six things your gift makes possible, in the order they compound.

  • Strengthen school leadership across government schools.
  • Build teachers' capacity through continuous professional development.
  • Expand Project Based Learning into more classrooms.
  • Support Teacher Education Institutes and the leaders who run them.
  • Strengthen community participation in children's education.
  • Drive research and innovation for system-wide improvement.
